# Sway 1.0.0

## The edges of your trackpad, as controls

Swipe along the side of your trackpad to change volume or brightness. Tap a corner to paste. Sway turns the edges you never use into the controls you reach for all day, with haptics, a native indicator, and almost no battery.

## It feels built in

- **49 actions** for any edge or tap: volume, brightness, keyboard backlight, microphone, media, seeking, tabs, windows, desktops, editing, clipboard history, Shortcuts, Night Shift, True Tone, extra dim, and more.
- **Fine control.** While swiping, slide inward from the edge to adjust in small steps, like scrubbing a video.
- **Speed-sensitive levels.** Slow swipes are precise; a quick flick covers more range.
- **Notches.** Levels rest briefly at 50% and at your favorite volume or brightness, with a crisp tap.
- **Haptics up to Strongest**, driving the trackpad's actuator directly for its firmest click.
- **An indicator that follows your finger**, hugging the screen edge you're touching.

## More from every edge

- **Taps and corners.** Double-tap or tap-and-hold any edge or corner. Corners can use one finger even when swipes use two. A resting palm never triggers a tap.
- **Layers.** Hold ⌥ while you swipe, or use the other finger count, for a second action on each side.
- **Top and bottom edges** for one-finger swipes.

## Clipboard history you play like an instrument

Double-tap a corner and a glass picker blooms open. Slide one finger to glide through your recent copies, then lift to paste. Slide up to cancel. Each item shows where it came from and when. History lives in memory only, never on disk, and skips anything a password manager marks.

## Knows what you're doing

- **App profiles** change only the edges you pick while an app is in front; a **docked profile** applies when an external display is connected.
- **Per-device volume limits** keep headphones quieter than speakers.
- **Brightness follows the pointer** to Apple displays and to monitors that accept DDC/CI on Apple silicon, and works with the lid closed.
- **Palm protection** checks every touch for intent, with a safe test that changes nothing while you practise.

## Thoughtful details

A calm five-step welcome, an Overview with a live trackpad map, an edge map you can summon with a shortcut, Now Playing with artwork, per-app volume, window tiling, scroll-to-adjust on the menu bar icon, the volume click, `sway://` links for Shortcuts, settings export and import, and smooth, Apple-style motion throughout, with Reduce Motion respected everywhere.

## Permissions and requirements

Sway asks only when a feature needs it: **Accessibility** for gestures, **System Audio Recording** for per-app volume (Sway never records, saves, or sends audio), and **Automation** for Music or Spotify in Now Playing (Sway never launches a player). Requires macOS 13 or later; per-app volume needs macOS 14.2. Universal for Apple silicon and Intel.

DDC monitor control isn't available on Intel Macs or through many docks, DisplayLink adapters and TVs. Night Shift, True Tone and multitouch use private macOS interfaces, checked before every use.
