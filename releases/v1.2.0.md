# Sway 1.2.0

## It feels like it reads your mind

- **Fine control.** While swiping, slide inward from the edge to adjust in small steps, like scrubbing a video. A soft tap tells you it's on.
- **Speed-sensitive levels.** Slow swipes are precise; a quick flick covers more of the range.
- **Notches.** Levels rest briefly at 50% and at your favorite volume or brightness, with a crisp tap, so you can find them without looking.
- **Follow finger indicator.** The level hugs the side of the screen that matches the edge you're touching, and moves with your finger.

## More from every edge

- **Taps and corners.** Double-tap or tap-and-hold any edge or corner. Both start with a short, still tap, so a resting palm never triggers them.
- **Layers.** Hold ⌥ while you swipe, or use the other finger count, for a second action on each side edge.
- **Bottom edge.** Swipe left or right along the bottom with one finger.
- **36 actions.** New: app volume, switch audio output, seek in track, show now playing, tile window, Night Shift warmth, True Tone, extra dim, Run a Shortcut, and the edge map. Custom shortcuts and Shortcut names are now recorded per edge.

## Knows what you're doing

- **App profiles** change only the edges you pick while an app is in front. A **docked profile** applies while an external display is connected.
- **Per-device volume limits** keep headphones quieter than speakers.
- **Brightness follows the pointer**, to Apple displays and to monitors that accept DDC/CI on Apple silicon. With the lid closed, a controllable external display is used.
- **Extra dim** continues below the lowest backlight level. Quitting Sway always restores normal brightness.

## Little things

- A **Now Playing** display with artwork for media gestures.
- A live trackpad map in Settings, and an **edge map** shortcut that shows what every edge does in the current app.
- **Scroll the menu bar icon** for volume, or with ⌥ for brightness, plus the familiar volume click.
- **Export and import** your settings. **sway://** links let Shortcuts pause Sway, set levels within your limits, or show the edge map.

## Permissions and limits

Sway asks only when you use a feature that needs it: **System Audio Recording** for app volume (Sway never records, saves, or sends audio) and **Automation** for Music or Spotify in Now Playing (Sway never launches a player). The app adds two documented entitlements for these, Apple Events and audio input; the release check rejects anything else.

DDC monitor control isn't available on Intel Macs or through many docks, DisplayLink adapters and TVs, and ambiguous multi-monitor setups are reported as unsupported. Night Shift and True Tone use private macOS interfaces, checked before every use. App volume needs macOS 14.2 or later. Existing assignments, the shared custom-shortcut pair, and your other settings are unchanged when updating. New feel options start on and can be turned off in Gestures and Haptics.

## Install or update

Download the universal DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel, or choose **Install update** in Sway's Software updates settings. This release remains self-signed and unnotarized, with Hardened Runtime enabled.
